Item of the Week: Busted Crown

Description | Anecdote | Fun Facts

Description

The item pictured above is a small crown that was offered in 2011 at the Bibbidi Bobbidi Boutique in Disney World. The tiara fits perfectly inside the palm of an adult hand. The structure of the crown is a steel material, with alternating blue and white crystals covering the front part of it. In the lower center of the crown, a blue Mickey Mouse head design is featured. It feels like a cheap children's toy.

I acquired the crown during my trip to Disney when I was 6 years old, as I went to the boutique myself and went through the small makeover package they offer there. It has sat on my shelf for years since then, and although it’s a cute memento, the crown is falling apart. It’s made of very flimsy material, has tarnished greatly on the back, and the crystals are beginning to fall out of the crown. https://disneyworld.disney.go.com/shops/magic-kingdom/bibbidi-bobbidi-boutique-park/

Anecdote

As stated, I got the crown during my first and only trip to Disney World.    I was 5 or 6 years old, and had woken up at what I thought was an egregiously early time to get to my appointment at the boutique. My younger sister and I were tired, but excited to get this magical makeover from what we believed were real fairy godmothers.

As we approached the salon, we got an incredible experience as the mascots for Mickey and Minnie appeared next to us and held our hands as they guided us towards the castle. Once inside, we got to decide on the colors of our tiaras, and once we chose we were taken off to get our hair done. It was a simple updo coated with glitter and hairspray, and the crown was firmly snapped in with the hair pins that used to be attached to it.

I wore it all day, and once I was back in the hotel room it was shoved into a random bag, and then proceeded to sit on my shelf for the past 15 years.

Fun Facts

The Bibbiddi Bobbiddi Boutique has been around for years, and over time the experience has evolved and changed. The crown I have represents one tier, but there are several different packages offered. Some focus on just hair and makeup, but others are hundreds of dollars and considered a luxury experience.
